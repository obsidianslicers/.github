# Obsidian Slicers

## About Us

In the chaotic expanse of the galaxy, there are those who crave power through discipline. Our master, Darth Drennan, first imposed his will by conceiving the Troop Tracker - a rigorous system designed to bring absolute order to the Florida Garrison. Recognizing the necessity of a controlled architect to house this power, Drennan subjugated the strategic mind of Stu Ellerbusch, binding him to his dominion. It was through the crucible of this forced alliance that the absolute command center, Obsidian Slicers, was forged. This dark nexus of technology and martial dedication ensures that every trooper is a disciplined asset, every mission is meticulously logged, and the Garrison's strength is quantifiable and absolute. Ellerbusch's scalable code and Data Specters now serve as the necessary infrastructure for our growing empire. Together, they stand not as equals, but as the relentless Guardians of Order, the unseen Keepers of the Roster, and the silent, terrifying engine that dictates the structure and success of the entire Legion.

## Mission Focus: Galactic Trooper Tracking Maintenance

We are an assembly of specialized minds dedicated to the necessary, often thankless task of maintaining and securing the **Empire's Troop Tracking System**. This organization exists solely for the efficient structuring and reliable deployment of essential troop‑movement data. We are the architects of the **Trooper Operations Array** — the clean, optimized front‑end and robust back‑end code that keeps the tracker operational and accurate.

### Our Mandate

Our mandate is simple: Maximum Code Integrity. Minimal Technical Debt (a consequence of volunteering). Such is the expectation of the Empire.

We operate with the precision of a targeting computer and the efficiency of a well‑drilled stormtrooper battalion. Our architecture is engineered for stability, our logic honed for reliability, and our interfaces forged for absolute clarity. We write the code, we refine the systems, and we ensure the tracker performs with unwavering obedience.

Our discipline is our strength.
Our conventions are our armor.
Our efficiency is the weapon that keeps the Empire's Troop Tracking System firmly under Imperial control.
